UMI-Anchored Effective Scaling With GSE164378

This tutorial fits the five-parameter effective model to RNA–ADT mutual information measured across UMI depths. One additional parameter, \(B_0\), then calibrates the cell-number response around the known reference point.

Empirical UMI calibration

At every UMI depth, empirical MI is calculated with all 4,000 cells and fixed rank-12 RNA and ADT subspaces. The effective aspect ratio uses the 2,000-feature RNA input dimension, \(d=2000\), rather than the retained MI rank.

umi_observed <- scscale_empirical_inu_grid(

Fit the effective model

The five fitted parameters are \[ (I_\infty,C,\theta_Y,a,b). \] The nonlinear optimizer varies only \((\theta_Y,a,b)\). For each optimizer evaluation, \((I_\infty,C)\) are solved by constrained weighted least squares. The effective signal remains linear, \(q(U)=a+bU\). Only its recoverability at the BBP boundary is smoothed: \[ \theta_X(n,U)=\frac{q(U)}{q(U)+1}G\!\left(q(U);d/n\right), \] \[ G(q;c)= \begin{cases} 0, & q^2\le c,\\ \exp\{-c/(q^2-c)\}, & q^2>c. \end{cases} \] This gate turns on smoothly at \(q=\sqrt{d/n}\), approaches the original strong-signal law, and introduces no new parameter.

fit <- scscale_effective_fit(

  d = effective_dimension,

summarize_mi <- function(data, x) {
  split_data <- split(data$I_empirical, data[[x]])
  data.frame(
    x = as.numeric(names(split_data)),
    I_mean = vapply(split_data, mean, numeric(1)),
    I_sd = vapply(split_data, sd, numeric(1))
  )
}

The reference count \(n_0=4000\) is fixed by the UMI experiment, not fitted. The cell-number data estimate one direct scale, \(B_0\), at the reference UMI depth \(U_0\). The original theoretical scale is \[ A_n(U)= \frac{\theta_Yd} {q(U)\{q(U)+1\}\{1-\theta_Yq(U)/(q(U)+1)\}}, \] and its relative UMI dependence transfers the fitted scale: \[ B(U)=B_0\frac{A_n(U)}{A_n(U_0)}. \] Let \(I_0(U)\) be the UMI-fit prediction at \(n_0\), translated by the single empirical anchor at \((n_0,U_0)\). The cell-number prediction is \[ I_B(n,U)=I_0(U)+C \log\left\{ \frac{1+B(U)/n_0}{1+B(U)/n} \right\}. \] All five UMI-fit parameters remain fixed. Only \(B_0\) is estimated from cell-number observations. Because only the ratio \(A_n(U)/A_n(U_0)\) is used, the arbitrary absolute scale of \(q(U)\) does not enter the cell calibration. No BBP indicator or additional gate is applied to this correction.
